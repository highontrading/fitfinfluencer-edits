# FitFinfluencer editing rules

These rules come from the creator and apply to **every video edited from now on** (from batch 5 onwards). Batches 1–4 stay as delivered.

## Voice (non-negotiable)

1. **Never change the original voice.** The lav-mic voice from the raw video goes into the edit as recorded:
   - no EQ, compression, de-noise, de-ess, limiter or loudness normalisation;
   - no pitch or formant change.
2. **The only allowed change is speed: 1.1x.** The video is sped up to 1.1x with pitch and formants preserved (the creator's choice, because he wants a fast pace).
3. Cut out fumbles, repeats and dead air, but never process what is kept.

## Takes

4. **Always use the LAST take.** When a line is said more than once, keep only the final attempt, even if an earlier one looks usable.

## Sound

5. **No background music.** None at all.
6. **Sound effects at 30%** of the previous level (×0.3 amplitude, about −10.5 dB). Pick sound effects that fit the mood of the video: subtle and weighty for serious topics, lighter for upbeat ones.

## Text

7. **Captions stay in Hinglish**, exactly as spoken.
8. **All other on-screen text is in English.** This covers titles, labels, stats, charts and calls to action. Never use Hindi or Hinglish for explanatory text.
9. **No emojis anywhere.** Use plain text, and show the real thing with real footage or images.
10. **Cinematic typography:**
    - some text sits *behind* the speaker (person cut-out);
    - text appears in different positions across the whole screen, so the eye keeps moving;
    - nothing should look like a basic template.

## Visuals

11. **Real visuals for every science point.** Show exactly what is being said:
    - heart, arteries or tissue on screen when he names them;
    - an ECG or heart-rate monitor for heart rate;
    - a real doctor for "doctor";
    - real gym footage for exercise.
12. **Use the creator's own B-roll first.** That means his gym footage and diet photos from his Google Drive. Use stock only when his own footage doesn't cover the point.
13. Stock must be licence-clean for commercial use:
    - Mixkit Free License;
    - Wikimedia Commons Public Domain, CC0 or CC BY.
    - Credit every clip in `STOCK_CREDITS.md`.

## Style and goal

14. **Edit to the video.** Watch the whole raw first. Understand its emotion, topic and seriousness, and match the edit to them.
15. **Retention first (MrBeast-style).** Hold attention every second:
    - frequent cuts, zoom-ins and zoom-outs;
    - B-roll on every concrete noun;
    - open loops and suspense so people stay to the end.
16. **Premium quality.** Every reel should look like a ₹1.5–2 lakh edit.
